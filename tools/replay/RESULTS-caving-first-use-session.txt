Two large gaps have direct first-use matches
-------------------------------------------
1. About80.8s after recorder start:125.371ms main-submit interval. The colored-star
   render path loads AtmosphericsStarScratch from the companion in16.942ms, then
   waits89.326ms in PipelineCache.get for minecraft:ambientfog/position_color_triangles.
   The backend MetalPipelineCompile span is19.328ms near the end of that wait.
   These are nested/overlapping measurements: do not add them, or call the whole
   89ms pure Metal/GPU compilation time. Upstream preparation/queueing contributes
   to the request's wait, but this capture does not isolate every component.

   Installed2.6.8 bytecode confirms SkyRenderingMixin.ambientfog$getPipeline lazily
   builds and caches ambientfog$pipeline on its first call. It does not register
   the object in the required-pipeline list. Its first actual draw therefore
   triggers compilation after resource loading, even while the player is caving.
   The shader is used by colored stars; the ambientfog location alone should not
   be interpreted as evidence that fog distance or haze blending caused the hitch.

   Actionable companion change: obtain that exact cached pipeline and include it
   in resource-reload compilation, preserving object identity, normal cache/reload
   ownership and blend/topology state. Prelink the companion's own scratch helper
   before gameplay as well. Do not alias it to a different pipeline by location
   or turn off stars. Keep this fix in Atmospherics Fixes, outside core mcopt.

2. About150.8s after start:136.638ms interval handling the first transient/moving
   block effect. Actual stable loads on the render thread:
     TransientBlock:12.293ms
     TransientBlockRenderState:8.769ms

   The installed game produces ClientboundAddTransientBlockPacket from
