====================================

------------------
An exact APFS copy of the current survival save was verified against a full-world
SHA-256 before launch. This was not terrain regenerated from its seed. The route
starts at the saved underground player position and traverses five parts of its
connected cavern, then returns. Offline saved-block analysis found56533 connected
dry, two-block-high cells and verified body clearance along every interpolated
segment. The camera covered approximately680 blocks in280 measured seconds after
20s warmup.28 route segments,16773 frame intervals, zero unfocused frames, zero
position error, stable settings; runner validForComparison=true.

The isolated pack used core fork.19 and separate Atmospherics Fixes0.1.3, the
current main pack's supporting mods/settings, render distance12, LOD256/Low/two
workers, VSync60,4GB ZGC and2880x1620 world target. Its copied same-world LOD data
was transplanted into the established owned save identity after verifying identical
generation settings; startup confirmed that exact identity was used. Source save,
cache and pack were read only. The main pack remains on fork.18/companion0.1.2.

The development harness's naturalCaves mode uses creative camera movement and
leaves the copied world's mob-spawning rule enabled; time/weather advancement is
frozen. Original blocks, player inventory and entities come from the save. No
synthetic room, scripted mining, forced particles or test-mob summons were used.
Flight does not reproduce survival combat AI or player mining inputs. The final
screenshot shows the actual torch-lit deepslate cave and original equipped items.

Important: presentation stalls survived the short game-loop intervals
------------------------------------------------------------------
Native presented timestamps show a583.332ms gap around measured42.25..42.83s,
plus100ms and83.333ms gaps later. The largest gap has independent acquisition
evidence, so it must not be dismissed as merely delayed JFR callback retirement:

- main submits3819..3833 had drawableStatus=2 (bounded wait expired), about22..24ms
  per attempt, and no drawable/presentation was scheduled for those15 frames;
- submit3834 recovered the single pending worker result:471.384ms elapsed inside
  acquisition, only0.091ms thread CPU; surface generation stayed1;
- before/after acquired drawables were scheduled and Java ownership released;
- the three preceding callbacks and the first recovered callback reported zero
  presentedTime, while the next valid timestamp ended the583ms gap;
- main GPU spans in that region were approximately8.7..36.8ms; Java continued
  committing frames, with no matching half-second GC/VM stop.

The native cadence probe uses fixed atomic slots, and its Java pending-observation
ring did not overflow. This is presentation starvation corroborated by failed
acquisition attempts, not evidence that the Java loop froze for583ms. It explains
why a loop/FPS-only metric can look improved while visible continuity still fails.
It does not identify whether the underlying cause is the driver, compositor,
system scheduling/residency or renderer interaction. Native acquisition wall time
is not CPU execution time, and GPU frame spans do not measure compositor work.

JVM resident samples averaged954MiB and peaked1164MiB. Global paging counters
advanced across launch-to-exit and machine CPU was higher than JVM CPU, especially
early; those system-wide snapshots cannot establish per-hitch causality. Do not
blame heap size, LOD generation, a browser or WindowServer from this capture alone.

Follow-up priorities
--------------------
1. Investigate drawable/presentation starvation with skipped-present and native
   worker timing alongside displayed cadence. Preserve the bounded wait's input
   responsiveness; reducing its timeout alone cannot make an unavailable drawable
   present. Rendering full frames during a long acquisition outage also deserves
   scrutiny because those frames cannot be displayed.
2. Address measured first-use texture/model helper costs through the normal
   resource lifecycle, without unbounded texture preloading or new long startup
   pauses. Existing falling-block/star fixes do not cover these paths.
3. Profile the sampled particle-blend and lighting-invalidation scopes separately
   before making a specific optimization claim. A later gameplay capture still
   needs actual digging/combat to exercise those critical inputs.
